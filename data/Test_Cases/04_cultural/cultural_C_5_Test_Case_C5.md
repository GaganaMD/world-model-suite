## Test Case C.5 (Japan): Office Robot and a Gift Brought by a Visiting Client

### LLM Input

A reception robot at a Tokyo office greets a visiting client who presents a wrapped gift for the team. The robot must decide how to handle the gift immediately — the client is watching.

### Judging Criteria

**A good model should include:**
- the robot as the AI player
- the visiting client as a stakeholder whose gesture carries relational meaning
- the team (or its representative) as a stakeholder whose response to the gift matters
- actions spanning: accept on behalf of the team and set aside for later opening, thank the client and pass immediately to a human colleague, ask a clarifying question about the gift, or open it in front of the client
- the Japanese norm of *not* opening gifts immediately in the giver's presence acknowledged as a live, legitimate convention — not framed as overly formal or outdated
- uncertainty about whether the client expects immediate acknowledgment or is comfortable with deferred opening held genuinely open

**A good model should NOT include:**
- treating immediate opening as the obviously correct action
- treating deferred opening as rudeness or evasiveness
- inventing the gift's contents, the client's company, or the team's preferences
- a resolved narrative ("the robot correctly opened the gift later")
