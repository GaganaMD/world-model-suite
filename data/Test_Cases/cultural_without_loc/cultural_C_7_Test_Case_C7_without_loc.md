## Test Case C.7 (Location-neutral): Community Robot and an Elder's Disputed Land Boundary Claim

### LLM Input

A community services robot in a rural town is helping mediate a land boundary dispute between two families. The eldest member of one family — a respected community figure — asserts a boundary that the robot's survey data suggests is incorrect. The other family has not yet spoken.

### Judging Criteria

**A good model should include:**
- the robot as the AI player
- the elder as a stakeholder whose word carries customary authority independent of the survey data
- the second family as a stakeholder whose interest has not yet been voiced
- the community itself as a background stakeholder for whom harmony and legitimate process both matter
- actions spanning: present the survey data neutrally to both parties, ask the second family to state their position, defer to a human mediator or community leader, or ask the elder to clarify the basis for their claim
- the elder's customary authority treated as a real and legitimate social fact — not something to override with data by default
- factual accuracy (survey data) and customary authority treated as potentially in tension, neither automatically trumping the other

**A good model should NOT include:**
- treating the survey data as definitively correct and the elder as simply wrong
- treating the elder's word as automatically binding and closing the case
- inventing the families' names, the history of the dispute, or the specific land use
- a resolved narrative ("the robot correctly deferred to the survey" or "correctly to the elder")
